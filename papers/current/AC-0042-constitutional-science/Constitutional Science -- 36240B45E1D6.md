## Abstract
Debates over the "scientific method" are often framed as philosophical disagreements about falsification, probability, or paradigm change. These disputes typically remain content-level arguments--what counts as evidence, how belief should update, or whether revolutions are rational. This paper proposes a structural reframing. When expressed in constitutional form, the Popperian, Bayesian, and Kuhnian formulations of scientific practice reveal themselves not as mutually exclusive doctrines, but as governance layers operating within a single epistemic system.

Each formulation locates authority differently: at the level of empirical integrity, probabilistic adjudication, or community standard evolution. Each encodes a distinct refusal discipline and distributes its oracle dependencies differently. By rendering these features structurally--through invariants, admissibility gates, oracle declarations, and outcome transitions--we expose complementary strengths and characteristic failure modes. The resulting insight is that scientific objectivity is not metaphysical purity but auditable dependency discipline. The scientific method, properly understood, is constitutional.

***
## 1. Introduction: From Method to Constitutional Placement

The phrase "the scientific method" suggests a unified procedure. Yet historical and contemporary practice reveals multiple competing formulations:

The falsification-centered approach associated with Karl Popper.

The probabilistic updating framework grounded in Bayesian inference.

The sociotechnical paradigm analysis articulated by Thomas Kuhn.

These are frequently presented as rival epistemologies. The debate is cast as a dispute over the correct philosophical account of science.

However, once expressed structurally--defined by invariants, admissibility gates, oracle declarations, and refusal semantics--the dispute reframes itself. The three formulations do not primarily disagree about evidence. They relocate epistemic authority.

Popper governs at the level of empirical integrity.
Bayes governs at the level of belief adjudication.
Kuhn governs at the level of communal standards and framework evolution.

The question is not which is correct in isolation. The question is where each places constitutional power within the scientific enterprise.

This paper does not argue for a victor. It clarifies the jurisdictional structure.

***
## 2. The Popperian Core: Integrity Through Refusal

The Popperian formulation privileges falsifiability. Structurally, it enforces:

Operationalizable claims.

Testable predictions under defined protocols.

Admissibility of disconfirming evidence.

Replication as status reinforcement.

Authority here is placed at the boundary of empirical integrity. A claim that cannot be tested cannot enter the jurisdiction of science.

The power of this structure lies in refusal discipline. It refuses unfalsifiable inputs. It insists that adjudication events be documented and replicable. Its backbone is constraint.

Yet this discipline introduces brittleness. Real-world models are approximate; confounds are complex; statistical noise blurs clean falsification. In practice, strict falsification often collapses into ritualized thresholding rather than decisive disproof.

Failure mode signature: rigidity under model ambiguity.

Popperian science locates constitutional authority in the integrity gate. It protects entry into the system.

***
## 3. The Bayesian Core: Adjudication as Policy

The Bayesian formulation reframes scientific reasoning as belief update. Structurally, it requires:

Explicit priors.

Declared likelihood models.

Replayable posterior updates.

Policy-defined decision thresholds.

Authority here shifts to adjudication mechanics. Evidence does not destroy hypotheses; it reshapes probability mass.

A central structural insight emerges:

Decision thresholds are policy, not mathematics.

A posterior probability does not compel status transition. A declaration of "confirmed" or "disconfirmed" requires an explicit decision rule--often tied to loss functions, cost asymmetries, or institutional norms. What appears purely statistical is constitutionally governed.

The strength of Bayesian structure lies in continuity. It accommodates uncertainty and partial evidence. It prevents premature binary collapse.

Yet it introduces a new vulnerability: mathematical formalism can obscure hidden modeling assumptions. Priors may be smuggled under neutrality. Likelihood functions may encode unexamined preprocessing choices.

Failure mode signature: formal rigor masking undeclared dependencies.

Bayesian science locates constitutional authority in the update rule and decision policy.

***
## 4. The Kuhnian Layer: Standards as Mutable Law

Kuhn's analysis shifts focus to the community level:

Paradigms define legitimate problems and exemplars.

Anomalies accumulate under normal science.

Crises destabilize confidence.

Revolutions restructure evaluative standards.

Authority here is placed at the level of communal norms and interpretive judgment.

Structurally, this introduces a decisive feature: standards drift. Evaluation criteria are not static invariants; they are versioned artifacts within communities.

The Kuhnian layer does not replace empirical adjudication. It governs the meta-conditions under which adjudication occurs. It describes how the constitution of science itself evolves.

However, without constraint, sociological dominance can impersonate epistemic authority. If community consensus overrides empirical integrity gates, constitutional collapse follows.

Failure mode signature: sociological authority substituting for evidentiary discipline.

Kuhn locates constitutional power at the level of standards governance.

***
## 5. Oracle Surface Analysis

An oracle, in governance terms, is an authority whose outputs are accepted without internal proof.

Scientific methods do not eliminate oracles. They distribute them differently:

Popperian: oracles appear in operational definitions and measurement calibration.

Bayesian: oracles appear in prior selection, likelihood modeling, and threshold choice.

Kuhnian: oracles appear in standard-setting bodies and interpretive judgments about crisis.

Objectivity is therefore not the absence of oracles. It is oracle visibility.

When priors are undeclared, when standards drift silently, when threshold policies masquerade as inevitabilities, the oracle surface becomes hidden. Authority becomes unaccountable.

The constitutional question is not whether judgment exists. It is whether judgment is declared and contestable.

***
## 6. Refusal Semantics as Constitutional Clause

Each formulation defines what the system must refuse:

Popper refuses unfalsifiable claims.

Bayesian refuses updates without declared priors and likelihoods.

Kuhnian refuses paradigm-neutral pretense.

Refusal semantics operate as constitutional clauses. They define admissibility boundaries.

When refusal collapses, dogma forms.
When refusal overextends, inquiry ossifies.

Balanced science requires:

1. Operational discipline (entry integrity).

2. Transparent update policy (adjudication).

3. Versioned standard governance (meta-level adaptation).

These are not rival principles. They are coordinated constitutional safeguards.

***
## 7. Replication Across Jurisdictions

Replication's meaning shifts with constitutional placement:

Under Popper, replication reinforces falsification resistance.

Under Bayes, replication reshapes posterior distributions.

Under Kuhn, replication patterns influence anomaly accumulation and paradigm stability.

The so-called "replication crisis" cannot be understood without specifying jurisdiction. A failure at one layer may signal instability in another.

Structural clarity prevents category error. It distinguishes empirical failure from adjudicative recalibration and from paradigm-level tension.

***
## 8. Incommensurability as Structured Delta

Kuhn's concept of incommensurability is often interpreted as epistemic rupture. Structurally expressed, it becomes a mapping problem:

Terms partially fail to translate.

Standards diverge.

Evaluation criteria mismatch.

Rather than mystifying the divide, we can record it as structured delta. Incommensurability becomes traceable difference rather than incomprehensible rupture.

This transforms paradigm comparison from philosophical impasse into governance mapping.

***
## 9. Science as Layered Constitutional Architecture

The comparative analysis reveals a composite constitutional structure:

Layer 1 -- Empirical Integrity
Operationalization, provenance, replication discipline.

Layer 2 -- Adjudicative Update
Belief revision under declared probabilistic or threshold rules.

Layer 3 -- Standards Governance
Community-level drift, anomaly management, and framework revision.

Confusion arises when one layer claims supremacy:

Falsification alone cannot govern sociotechnical evolution.
Probabilistic updating cannot define communal legitimacy.
Sociological description cannot replace empirical integrity.

Scientific resilience emerges when these jurisdictions remain distinct yet coordinated.

***
## 10. Objectivity as Auditable Humility

Objectivity is often imagined as detachment from human influence.

Structural comparison suggests a different formulation:

Objectivity is auditable humility.

It consists of:

Declared dependencies.

Receipted adjudications.

Transparent decision thresholds.

Explicit oracle references.

Structured refusal and defer pathways.

Just as constitutional democracies rely on recorded deliberation rather than divine right, scientific objectivity relies on documented process rather than metaphysical neutrality.

Objectivity is not the elimination of judgment. It is judgment constrained by declared procedure.

***
## 11. Implications Beyond Science

When expressed constitutionally, epistemologies become comparable governance systems.

Legal systems can be examined for hidden oracles.
Economic models can be evaluated for threshold governance.
Political systems can be inspected for silent standard drift.

Disagreement at the level of belief often masks disagreement at the level of constitutional placement.

Clarifying jurisdiction clarifies conflict.

***
## 12. Conclusion

The Popperian, Bayesian, and Kuhnian formulations of scientific method do not compete as alternative truths about science. They describe distinct governance regimes operating at different layers of epistemic practice.

From their structural comparison, several insights follow:

Science always contains policy decisions.

Oracles cannot be eliminated; they must be declared.

Replication, falsification, and probabilistic updating are complementary mechanisms.

Paradigm shifts are governed events, not mystical ruptures.

Objectivity is procedural accountability, not metaphysical neutrality.

Science is not a single method. It is a layered constitutional system of admissibility, adjudication, and standards evolution.

Its resilience does not arise from the absence of human judgment.

It arises from forcing judgment to declare its authority.
