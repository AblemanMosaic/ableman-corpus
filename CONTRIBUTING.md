# Contribution Boundary

The Ableman Corpus is an authored research collection. Repository issues and proposed corrections may identify defects, missing citations, broken links, rendering problems, or potential connections. They do not modify a paper's standing, authorship, semantic identity, or current edition by themselves.

Changes to paper prose require author review. Software contributions should preserve the source/projection distinction, representation-specific hashes, detached receipts, mixed-license boundary, and one-current-edition policy.

Never commit provider credentials, access tokens, local databases, unpublished provider-control state, or private evidence packages.
